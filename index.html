    Setting up the automation
    1. Pick your neighbourhoods on the map or in the list.
    2. Click Save to GitHub. It copies your list and opens the GitHub editor for favourites.json: select everything, paste and commit. The page shows the new list once GitHub Pages redeploys (about a minute).
    3. Create a Telegram bot with @BotFather. Add its token and your chat and group ids as the TOKEN, CHAT_ID and GROUP_ID repository secrets.
    4. Enable the Check Pararius workflow under Actions. It checks hourly, posts new listings in your zip codes to the group, and sends problems to your private chat.
